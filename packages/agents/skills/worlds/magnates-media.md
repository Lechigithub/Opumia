# MagnatesMedia film worlds, taken apart by eye (build list 6, D1)

Method: 30 shots spread over the whole film (`scratchpad/worldsheet.py`), read by eye in the session. Each entry: what
the world is made of, its own devices, and what it shares with the channel (editing, not world). Never a template.

## Ponzi: The Financial Idiot Who Scammed the World (MM-20221214), 1920s Boston, 115 shots
- **Made of:**
  - Ponzi's own sepia portraits (split face, close-ups);
  - 1920s dollar bills and banknotes, newspapers ("In July 1920 the Post presented");
  - bank buildings;
  - Calvin Coolidge;
  - smoke and clouds;
  - modern analogies for the mechanism (laptop, ShipStation, a parcel taped shut);
  - movie clips as performed reenactment (a crowd applauding, a man in a crowd).
- **Its own devices:**
  - pictogram explainers (a red bank with a green coin badge, yellow coin stacks, people icons, a dashed line);
  - a neon script title "Ponzi Scheme" on a blue circuit board;
  - a bank drawn as a ghost on white.
- **Words:** yellow serif and script over pictures, some white sans captions, few words per frame.
- **Palette:** sepia and black, punctuated by saturated orange (fire), neon yellow-green, blue.

## The Man Who Robbed An Entire Country (MM-20220930), 1MDB / Jho Low, 774 shots

    red carpet, parties, fireworks;

  - Hollywood film clips (The Wolf of Wall Street, The Great Gatsby);

- **Palette:** saturated night purple, teal, gold; glossy and colourful (the money is spent on screen).

## The Scammer Who Sold The Eiffel Tower (Twice) (MM-20221231), 1920s Paris, 362 shots

  - mugshot-style black-and-white group photos.
- **Its own device:** red collage cards with torn photo cut-outs and a yellow "?" ("Married a woman named Roberta" in

## What the three share (the channel's style, allowed to be shared)
- Film and TV drama clips used as performed reenactment. **We cannot do this:** owner rule "scripted dramas, film clips
  and trailers are never press". The gap goes to staged stock reenactment with anonymous actors and real period
  footage. It is a real limit on reaching the channel's look and is tracked in BUILD-LIST-6.
- Real people's own photos at the centre of the story.
- Few words per frame, one device per film for words.
- Dark frames and fades between sections.
- Palette changes per film.

## What none of them share
Era, places, objects, materials, the word device, the palette. Three scam stories, three worlds.

**Across all 37:**
- the world is read off the subject's own brand, era, places and objects, without exception;
- each film has one word device of its own (lowercase serif, terminal brackets, Pepsi waves, Chanel pink panels,
  brick-built titles, typewriter);
- documents and screens appear only where the story is about documents (privacy, a strike, a certificate);
- most of every film is footage of people: drama clips playing the story (we cannot), staged stock, and real archive.

The end card is the channel's own branding and is never copied.

## The principle (what makes every world different, read across all 37 films, no exception)
**A film dresses in its subject's own visual language and era.**
- Red Bull looks like Red Bull's events.
- Silk Road looks like a darknet terminal.
- Nike is purple with sneakers as the chart.
- DeLorean is 1970s Kodak film.
- Ponzi is 1920s newspapers and banknotes.
- Uber is its own black brand on crumpled paper.
- Hugo Boss is gold serif luxury over a sepia past.
- Google+ is built in Google's four colours.
- LEGO's titles are built from bricks.
- 1MDB is the luxury the money bought.

The world is not chosen from a menu: it is read off the story's era, brand, places, objects and materials. The word
device and palette follow from that. This is the rule the art director's world bible starts from.
